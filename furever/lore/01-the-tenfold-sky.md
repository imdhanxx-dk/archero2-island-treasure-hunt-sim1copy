# I. THE TENFOLD SKY

## The mistake everyone makes

Ask any child in Mirrowen what the Ten Stars are and they will tell you:
*powers.* Fire, water, wind, growth. Ten buckets of magic that the world was
conveniently divided into.

That is not what they are.

The Ten Stars are not **nouns**. They are **verbs**.

Each Star is not a *substance* the world is made of. It is a *thing the world
does to itself* — one of ten motions that, taken together, are the whole of how
Veylora stays alive. Fire is not the Ember Star's domain. **Transformation** is.
Fire is merely the loudest way transformation announces itself.

This is why a Star cannot simply be pointed at a problem. You do not aim a verb.
You conjugate it.

| Star | What the books say | What it actually is | Homeland |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Meadow** | growth, flowers, healing | **CONTINUE** — succession, decay, the passing-on of life through other life | Whispering Meadow |
| **Moon** | dreams, illusion, instinct | **INTERPRET** — the act of a mind turning sensation into meaning | Moonlit Woods |
| **Crystal** | water, ice, reflection | **KEEP** — structure, record, the lattice that holds a shape against time | Crystal Coast |
| **Ember** | fire, heat, volcanoes | **CHANGE** — irreversible transformation; the door that does not open backward | Ember Canyon |
| **Sky** | wind, storms, lightning | **CARRY** — circulation; the fact that nothing stays where it happened | Skygarden Peaks |
| **Neon** | bioluminescence, electricity | **SIGNAL** — one living thing making itself known to another | Neon Rainforest |
| **Frost** | snow, cold, auroras | **HOLD STILL** — preservation; the refusal of decay | Frostfang Valley |
| **Light** | *(redacted)* | **SHOW** — revelation; making a thing available to be seen | Celestial Ruins |
| **Shadow** | *(redacted)* | **WITHHOLD** — contrast, rest, concealment; the ground against which anything can be perceived at all | Eclipse Sanctuary |
| **Celestial** | *(redacted)* | **RELATE** — the connective tissue; the claim that the other nine were never separate | Astral Wilds |

Read that column of verbs downward and something uncomfortable emerges.

*Continue. Interpret. Keep. Change. Carry. Signal. Hold still. Show. Withhold.
Relate.*

That is not a list of elements.

That is a description of **a mind**.

Veylora's oldest scholars noticed this and were frightened enough to stop
writing about it. The note survives in only one hand, in the margin of a
water-ruined ledger in the Celestial Ruins:

> *We did not find ten forces in the world. We found ten things the world does,
> and we are one of the things it does them with.*

## The Law of Listening

The single most misunderstood fact about the Ten: **they do not create.**

A Meadow Star cannot make a dead forest live. An Ember Star cannot set fire to
something that will not burn. A Moon Star cannot put a dream inside a thing that
does not dream.

The Stars **amplify what is already trying to happen.** They are not engines.
They are the moment a whisper becomes audible.

The Law has three clauses, and Starkeeper apprentices learn them before they
learn to walk a Star across a room.

### 1. CONSENT — *a Star amplifies only what already wants to occur*

There must be a tendency present, however faint. A seed with the will to open.
A mind with the shape of a dream already in it. A stone already fracturing
along a line it chose a thousand years ago.

This is why a Star in the hands of a cruel person is far less useful than the
cruel person expects, and far more dangerous than they can survive. Cruelty
wants to force. The Stars do not know how to force. What they know how to do is
find the thing that *already* wanted to happen — and if you are angry enough,
the thing that already wanted to happen is often inside **you**.

Noctra learned this the hard way, and it is the reason for the cracks.

### 2. COMPATIBILITY — *a Star can only speak where its verb makes sense*

Every Star has a grammar. Put a Star somewhere its verb cannot be conjugated and
nothing happens — no failure, no backlash, simply silence, the way a question
in the wrong language is not wrong but merely unheard.

This is also why the homelands exist. A region is not a Star's *birthplace*.
It is the place where that Star's verb is **most legible** — where the world is
already doing that thing so loudly that the Star barely has to speak at all.

### 3. COST — *amplification is always borrowed*

This is the clause the Long Bright forgot.

Nothing is added to the world by a Star. Volume is moved. Every bloom the
Meadow Star lifts is paid for by a decay it accelerated somewhere out of sight.
Every fire Ember calms is a fire that will find another canyon.

Starkeepers call this **the Ledger**, and reading it is most of the job. A
Keeper who cannot find where the cost landed has not *avoided* the cost. They
have only failed to look.

> *"There are no free verbs."*
> — the first line of the Keeper's primer, written for children

## HOMING

Every Star remembers where it came from.

Not sentimentally. **Navigationally.**

If a Star ever comes to believe that it is about to be *captured* — not
carried, not asked, but taken and used — it will drop everything and go home.
Instantly. Through stone, through ocean, through whatever is in the way.

The scholars who studied this called the ability **Homing**, and mapped it to
four cues the Stars read simultaneously. Every one of them is borrowed from the
way real creatures cross oceans without a map.

**THE GRAIN** — the pull of Veylora's own magnetic field, read as a texture
running through the world like grain through wood. The Stars do not see it.
They lean on it, the way you lean on a slope you cannot see in the dark.

**THE TURNING** — the rotation of the night sky. Not any single star, but the
*pivot* — the one point everything else wheels around. This one must be
*learned*, and a very young Star, freshly formed, will home badly or not at all
until it has watched enough nights to find the still point.

**THE ANGLE** — the sun's height, corrected for the hour. Requires an internal
clock. A Star kept too long in darkness loses this cue first, which matters
enormously for the Shadow Star and for anything sealed underground.

**THE SCENT-MEMORY** — the chemical signature of the homeland itself. The
mineral taste of Crystal Coast's water. The specific rot of Whispering Meadow's
undersoil. This is the cue of last resort, the one that works when everything
else has failed, and it is the reason a Star can always find its way home even
if it has forgotten *why* it is going.

Lumi, it will turn out, has all four. He does not know it yet.

## DEEP-ROOTING — the part the legends leave out

Everyone tells the story of the Ten Stars flying home as though it were a
triumph. A beautiful escape. Ten lights tearing through the roof of the Vault
and crossing the world.

Nobody tells the next part.

**A Star that homes does not arrive and wait politely.**

Homing is not travel. It is *retreat*. The Star drops out of relation with
everything — including its Keeper, including the region's inhabitants,
including any voice that might moderate it — and sinks into the deep structure
of its homeland. Into roots. Into bedrock. Into the ice.

And there, with nothing to listen to, it does the only thing left.

**It does its verb. Forever. Without a reason.**

An un-moderated Star is not evil. It is worse than evil — it is *unattended*.
A verb with no sentence around it.

- **CONTINUE**, unattended, is a meadow that will not let anything die. Grass
  grows through the lungs of the things that eat it. Nothing rots. Nothing has
  room.
- **HOLD STILL**, unattended, is a valley where the snow that fell on the
  morning of the Vault has still not melted, and neither has anything else, and
  the elders there have not aged and cannot die and are very tired.
- **INTERPRET**, unattended, is a wood where everyone is dreaming and no one has
  agreed to wake, and the meanings have begun leaking between sleepers.
- **CHANGE**, unattended, is a canyon that will not finish becoming something.

This is the engine of Lumi's journey and the ugly joke at the centre of it:
**the Stars fled to protect themselves, and in protecting themselves they broke
ten regions.** They knew they would. They did it anyway. That fact will need
explaining before the story is over, and the explanation is not a comfortable
one.

Deep-Rooting also means a Star cannot simply be picked up. It must be *drawn
back into relation* — which is to say, it must be given a reason to listen
again. In practice this means someone has to go there, stand in the distortion,
and be worth answering.

That is the entire job of a Starkeeper, and it has never once been done with a
weapon.

## THE CONVOCATION

All ten Stars, in one place, at one time.

Common belief: this grants unimaginable power.

Common belief is wrong, and dangerously so.

A Convocation grants **consensus**.

When all ten verbs are conjugated in the same sentence, Veylora briefly stops
being a place where things are *true or false* and becomes a place where things
are **agreed**. What the Convocation settles on becomes what the world
remembers — in stone, in root, in water, and in every mind that lives in it.

It does not change the past. It changes **the record of the past**, everywhere,
at once, including inside people. And to the world, and to everyone in it,
there is no detectable difference between those two things.

Three Convocations are known.

**THE FIRST** — the Naming. Ten regions given their boundaries and their
grammar. Universally recorded, universally celebrated, carved on every
municipal building in Mirrowen.

**THE SECOND** — the end of the Grey Century, when a blight had eaten eight
regions and the Stars were gathered to agree, collectively, that Veylora was a
place where things grow. Recorded. Celebrated. Slightly less carved.

**THE THIRD** — the Redaction.

Not recorded.

Because it was the Convocation that did the recording.

## WHY THE BOOKS SAY SEVEN

Here is the sequence, and it is important that it is a *sequence*, because
almost everyone in Veylora believes it was three separate tragedies.

It was one act with three casualties.

**First, Shadow was sealed.** The vote of the First Eclipse. Shadow was declared
a corruption in the Tenfold Sky and removed from it — bound, with its Keeper,
beneath Eclipse Sanctuary.

**Second, Light was used.** To remove a thing from history you need more than a
chisel; you need every copy, every carving, every ledger, every mind. Only one
Star can make a thing *available to be seen* — and therefore only one Star can
make a thing *unavailable*. Light was turned around and pointed backward.
Statues went blank. Ink lifted off vellum. The name went out of use in a single
season, and people did not notice it going, which is the part that should
frighten you.

Light did what was asked. Light is the star of revelation, and it had been made
into an instrument of concealment.

Then Light left — not to a homeland it had ever used, but to the oldest ruin it
could find, the one place already full of things nobody could read. It has not
answered anyone since. The Celestial Ruins are not where Light was born. They
are where Light went to hide from what it had done.

**Third, Celestial had nowhere to stand.** The Celestial Star's verb is RELATE.
Its entire existence is the assertion that the other nine are one system
wearing ten masks. In a Tenfold Sky that had just amputated two of its own
members and called itself whole, the sentence Celestial exists to say had
become **false**.

So Celestial crossed the boundary of the known world into the Astral Wilds,
which is less a place than a direction, and the maps of Veylora have had an
edge ever since.

Seven remained. Seven were taught. Seven appear in the primers, on the temple
friezes, in the children's counting rhymes.

And every scholar who has ever noticed that seven is a strange number for a
system built on tens has been told the same thing, in the same slightly bored
voice, for four hundred years:

> *"It has always been seven."*

Which is precisely what a Convocation is for.

## THE THING THE STARS HAVE NEVER TOLD ANYONE

The Ten are alive.

Not "alive" as a figure of speech. Not "alive" the way a river is said to be
alive. They have preferences. They have grudges. They have, in at least two
documented cases, **lied**.

They allowed themselves to be gathered twice.

The third time, they were not asked.

And that, far more than the seal, far more than Noctra, far more than four
centuries of scrubbed stone, is the wound the Ten Stars are still carrying when
the story begins.

When Lumi's scarf broke open in the Star Vault, the Stars did not flee **from
Noctra**.

They fled from **being gathered again**.

He simply happened to be standing there.
