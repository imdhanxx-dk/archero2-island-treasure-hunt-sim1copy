# V. THE TEN REGIONS

Each region is a Star's homeland — the place where its verb is already so loud
that the Star barely has to speak.

Each is now **Deep-Rooted**: the Star has sunk into the bedrock and is doing its
verb forever, with nothing to listen to and no one to moderate it.

The pattern for every region is the same, and it should be the same, because the
repetition is the argument:

> **The Star is not attacking anyone. The Star is helping, at maximum volume,
> with no way to stop.**

---

## 1. WHISPERING MEADOW — *CONTINUE*

**Star:** Meadow. **Distortion:** nothing is permitted to end.

Endless green under a sky that never fully clouds. Grass to the shoulder. Every
flower open at once, including species that should be six months apart.

And nothing rots.

Fallen trees sit intact for years, green shoots driving up through them, holding
them together. The soil has no give — it is solid with roots that will not
release their hold. A creature that dies in Whispering Meadow is quietly, gently
grown *through*, and does not decompose, and is still there.

The farmers cannot clear a field. The fruit does not fall; it hangs, ripens
past ripeness, and stays. There is no room. There is so much life that there is
no room for life.

**The local problem:** a village that cannot bury its dead, and has stopped
talking about it.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *growth is not healing.* Healing requires a wound to
close and the tissue to change. A thing that only ever grows is not getting
better. It is getting bigger.

---

## 2. MOONLIT WOODS — *INTERPRET*

**Star:** Moon. **Distortion:** everyone is dreaming and the dreams have begun
to leak.

Silver birch, black water, permanent 3 a.m. The inhabitants are not asleep in
any ordinary sense — they walk, talk, cook, greet you politely. They are simply
*interpreting* continuously, and the raw material has run out, so they have
begun borrowing each other's.

Two villagers share a childhood. Neither had it. A mother describes her son's
first word and the words she uses are yours.

The danger is not monsters. The danger is that Moonlit Woods is very pleasant
and offers Lumi something no other region can: **a past.** Any past. As much as
he wants, in vivid detail, immediately.

**The local problem:** a girl who has been dreaming her drowned brother alive
for a year and has convinced the whole village to remember him with her.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *a memory you chose is not a memory.* The value of
his own past is not that it is pleasant. It is that it is **his**, and he did
not get a vote on it.

---

## 3. CRYSTAL COAST — *KEEP*

**Star:** Crystal. **Distortion:** the record has become more real than the
thing recorded.

A shoreline of impossible lattices, tidal flats gone to glass, waves that
crystallise mid-break and hang there. The city of Sealume is half-transparent.

Crystal keeps. Perfectly. Every event on the coast is now recorded in the
mineral structure — an exact and permanent archive of everything that happens,
readable by anyone who learns the trick.

The inhabitants have stopped remembering things, because why would you. It is
all written down. They consult the coast to find out what their childhood was.
They consult the coast to find out whether they were happy.

And they have learned, slowly, that **a perfect record of a life is not a life.**
The archive contains every word of every argument and not one reason why either
of them cared.

**The local problem:** a lighthouse keeper who reads the same recorded evening
every night and has not made a new memory in nine years.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *a record is not the truth; it is a shape the truth
left behind.* Which is, incidentally, the exact distinction Noctra has stopped
being able to make.

**Note:** a flawless crystal holds no information. Structure carries meaning
*through its flaws.* The most valuable object on Crystal Coast is a cracked one.

---

## 4. EMBER CANYON — *CHANGE*

**Star:** Ember. **Distortion:** nothing is allowed to finish becoming
something.

Red rock, black glass, heat rising in sheets. Fires that have burned for a year
without fuel. The canyon is mid-transformation everywhere and completes nothing
— stone half-melted, ash that will not settle, new land forming and unforming.

But Ember Canyon is not a wasteland, and the region must never be written as
one. The ash is the most fertile soil in Veylora. There are trees here whose
cones will not open without fire — they have waited their entire lives for the
thing everyone else calls destruction. The canyon-folk are not victims of the
fire. They **farm** it, and they are furious at outsiders who call their home
dead.

The problem is not that things burn. It is that nothing gets to be *finished*
burning and start again.

**The local problem:** a forge-town waiting for a harvest that cannot come,
because the burn never ends and the seed cones never get the signal.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *transformation requires the old thing to actually be
gone.* You cannot become something while still holding everything you were.

**And this is where Noctra breaks**, because this is the region whose verb is
the one he has refused for four hundred years.

---

## 5. SKYGARDEN PEAKS — *CARRY*

**Star:** Sky. **Distortion:** everything arrives everywhere.

Terraced mountains, hanging gardens, bridges in cloud, the great circulation of
Veylora's weather visible overhead as a slow wheel.

Sky carries. Now it carries *everything*, indiscriminately, at speed. Ash from
Ember Canyon falls on Frostfang. Spores from the Meadow choke the terraces. A
word spoken on one peak arrives on another three days later, weathered but
audible, and the Peaks have developed elaborate etiquette about this.

There is no such thing as a local event in Skygarden.

**The local problem:** two settlements at war over things they said in private
forty years ago, which arrived last spring.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *nothing stays where it happened.* Which is
comforting when he thinks about his small kindnesses, and terrible when Noctra
points out that it applies equally to twelve people voting in a warm room.

**The grounding image:** dust from a desert crossing an ocean to fertilise a
rainforest. The two places have never met and one of them is feeding the other.

---

## 6. NEON RAINFOREST — *SIGNAL*

**Star:** Neon. **Distortion:** everything is shouting and nothing is heard.

Bioluminescence at saturation. Every organism lit, pulsing, flashing — fungal
mats, root networks, insects, the rivers themselves. Beautiful beyond
description and completely unreadable, because signal requires contrast and
there is no longer any dark to signal *against*.

The fireflies here flash in dialects. The colonies coordinate by a chemical vote
— when enough of them agree, they all light at once. Now they never stop
agreeing, so the vote means nothing.

The forest is screaming *I am here* in ten thousand overlapping voices and not
one of them is reaching anybody.

**The local problem:** a species that cannot find mates any more, and a
researcher who has been trying to reintroduce darkness in a single valley and
being prevented by people who think she is trying to bring back the monster.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *to be heard, something must be quiet.* A signal is
made of its silences.

**This is the region that indicts the Long Bright directly,** and the first place
Lumi begins to suspect that Noctra might be right about something.

---

## 7. FROSTFANG VALLEY — *HOLD STILL*

**Star:** Frost. **Distortion:** nothing is permitted to change or decay,
including grief.

A valley under aurora, permanently. The snow that fell on the night of the Star
Vault has not melted. Neither has anything else.

Food does not spoil. Wounds do not close — but they do not worsen. The elders do
not age. They also do not die, and several of them very much want to, and this
is discussed openly and without melodrama, which makes it land harder.

Everything in Frostfang is exactly as it was, and the inhabitants have built an
entire culture of *waiting* around it.

**The local problem:** a family keeping a vigil over someone who has been
neither alive nor dead for four months, because Frost will not let go and they
have stopped being sure they want it to.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *preservation and love are not the same thing.* You
can hold something so still that you prevent it from ever being finished with.

**And this is where Lumi says the cruellest true thing in the story**, and it is
not aimed at an enemy. It is aimed at someone he has started to care about.

---

## 8. CELESTIAL RUINS — *SHOW*

**Star:** Light. **Distortion:** none. Light is not deep-rooted. Light is
**hiding.**

Vast, broken, gold-and-white architecture half-swallowed by cloud and time. The
oldest structures in Veylora and the emptiest.

Every surface here was written on. Every surface here is now blank — not worn,
not weathered. **Bleached.** Smooth stone where four thousand years of history
used to be, because this is where the Redaction was performed and the instrument
never left the scene.

The Light Star is here, awake, lucid, and refusing. It knows what it did. It was
the Star of revelation and it was used to conceal, and it has spent four hundred
years in a building full of blank walls it made blank.

It is the only Star that will not simply ask Lumi a question. It will **beg him
not to take it**.

**The local problem:** there are no locals. Nobody lives here. That is the
problem.

**The truth Lumi learns:** *being used to do a terrible thing is not the same as
being terrible* — and refusing to ever act again is not atonement, it is just a
second absence.

---

## 9. ECLIPSE SANCTUARY — *WITHHOLD*

**Star:** Shadow. **Distortion:** it is not deep-rooted either. It is
**sealed**, inside a person, exactly where it was put.

Black basalt galleries under a dead mountain. No light has entered in four
centuries. The dark here is not an absence — it has texture, weight, grain. This
is where Eclipse-silk is spun, and the looms are still standing, and one of them
still has work on it.

There are scratch-marks on one wall. Tally marks. A very great many of them,
and then a long stretch of none, and then they start again.

**The local problem:** the only inhabitant.

**The truth Lumi learns:** everything. This is where the ninth memory returns
and the whole story turns over.

---

## 10. ASTRAL WILDS — *RELATE*

**Star:** Celestial. **Distortion:** it is not a place and cannot be entered by
walking.

Beyond the edge of every map. Not distance — *dimension*. The Astral Wilds is
where Veylora keeps the fact that it is one thing.

From inside it, the other nine regions are visible simultaneously, and visibly
**connected**: the ash from Ember falling on Frostfang; the meltwater from
Frostfang feeding Crystal Coast; the spores from the Meadow riding Sky's
circulation to the Rainforest; the dark of Eclipse making the Neon legible; the
Moon's rhythms setting the Meadow's flowering and the Coast's tides.

Nine verbs, one sentence.

**The truth Lumi learns:** there were never ten forces. There is one process, and
ten ways of standing close enough to see part of it.

Which raises the question the Celestial Star will actually ask him, and it is the
question the entire game has been building to:

> *"Now that you know we are one thing — why do you still want ten?"*
