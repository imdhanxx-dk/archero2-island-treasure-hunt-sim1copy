# VIII. THE TEN MEETINGS

*The dialogue arc between Noctra and Lumi.*

One encounter per region. Noctra never fights him after the Vault — not once.
He follows. He argues. He waits.

The rule that generates every scene: **Noctra is the only creature alive who can
answer every question Lumi has, and telling him is useless.** A told fact is a
sentence about a stranger. Memory returns by address, not by information.

So Noctra has four hundred years of answers and no way to deliver them, and he
has to walk behind a cat who does not know him and watch him work it out.

The arc of the argument, meeting by meeting:

> *You were forgotten too* → *so was I, and look what it made me* →
> *then unmake it* → *I can't* → *then let someone else remember for you.*

---

## MEETING I — WHISPERING MEADOW
### *Growth is not healing*

*Lumi sits at the edge of a field that will not stop. A fallen tree, three years
down, still green, held upright by the grass growing through it. Behind him,
where there was nothing, there is now something very large.*

> **NOCTRA:** You don't remember me.

> **LUMI:** *(not turning around)* Should I?

> **NOCTRA:** ...
> *(a sound that is almost a laugh and does not get there)*
> No. That's the joke.

> **LUMI:** Is it a good joke?

> **NOCTRA:** It's a four-hundred-year joke. The setup was very long.

*Lumi finally turns. He is not afraid, which is itself alarming.*

> **LUMI:** You're the thing from the ceiling.

> **NOCTRA:** I'm the *what?*

> **LUMI:** I've got — there's a thing I keep getting. Black shape. Purple eyes.
> Stars going through a roof. It's the only piece I've got and it's got you in
> it, so, congratulations, you're my entire past.

> **NOCTRA:** *(quietly)* That's closer to true than you know.

> **LUMI:** Then tell me.

> **NOCTRA:** No.

> **LUMI:** *(standing up)* No? You came all this way to say *no*?

> **NOCTRA:** I came to see whether it was permanent.
> *(looking past him at the strangling field)*
> And because I wanted to see this. Look at it. It's doing everything right.
> Every blade of it is alive. Nothing here has died in a year.
> They're starving.

> **LUMI:** ...Yeah. The village can't clear a field. And they can't bury
> anyone, because the ground won't — the roots don't let go.

> **NOCTRA:** Growth isn't healing, Keeper.
> Healing needs a thing to close and change into a different thing. What you're
> looking at is a wound that decided to keep growing instead.

> **LUMI:** That sounds like something someone taught you.

> **NOCTRA:** *(a long pause)*
> It's something I taught someone.

> **LUMI:** Who?

> **NOCTRA:** *(already walking into the grass)* He forgot.

> **LUMI:** *(calling after him)* That's not — hey! That's not an answer!

> **NOCTRA:** *(not turning)* No. It's a wound that kept growing.

## MEETING II — MOONLIT WOODS
### *A memory you chose is not a memory*

*Black water. Silver birch. Permanent three in the morning. Lumi has just come
out of the girl's house, where a drowned brother was making tea.*

> **NOCTRA:** She'll never let him go.

> **LUMI:** *(sitting down hard on a root)* She knows. That's the worst part.
> She knows and she'd rather have the dream.

> **NOCTRA:** Wouldn't you?

> **LUMI:** *(immediately)* Yes.
> *(beat)*
> Obviously yes. Moon offered me a childhood on the way in. A whole one. Warm
> house, someone singing, the works. It was *right there*.

> **NOCTRA:** *(very carefully)* And?

> **LUMI:** And I didn't take it.

> **NOCTRA:** Why not?

> **LUMI:** Because I'd know.
> *(he pulls the empty scarf through his paws)*
> It'd be perfect and I'd know I picked it, and then every time it made me
> happy I'd remember I was the one who ordered it. A memory you chose isn't a
> memory. It's a preference wearing a costume.

*Noctra does not answer for a long time. When he does, it is not the voice he
has been using.*

> **NOCTRA:** I need to tell you something, and I want you to understand that I
> am not doing it to be cruel.

> **LUMI:** That's a fantastic start.

> **NOCTRA:** I have a memory. One night. Twelve people in a warm room and a
> woman reading words off a page.
> I have taken it out and looked at it every day for four hundred years.

> **LUMI:** ...Okay.

> **NOCTRA:** Do you know what remembering does to a memory?

> **LUMI:** Keeps it?

> **NOCTRA:** *Wears* it.
> Every time you pick it up you put it back down slightly differently. And then
> you remember the putting-down. And then you remember *that*.
> I have handled that night ten thousand times. There is nothing left of the
> original. There is only the ten-thousandth copy of a copy, and I made every
> single one of them, in the dark, with nothing to check it against.

> **LUMI:** So what's in it now?

> **NOCTRA:** *(and this is the first completely honest thing he says to him)*
> In the early copies, she wept.
> In the recent ones she doesn't look up.
> I don't know which one happened. I have no way to find out.
> I have been *angry* for four centuries at a face I am no longer certain I
> saw.

*Lumi looks at the girl's lit window. Then at him.*

> **LUMI:** ...Then you've got the same thing as me.

> **NOCTRA:** I have nothing in common with you.

> **LUMI:** You've got a memory you can't trust and I've got none at all. That's
> the same problem with different furniture.
> *(getting up)*
> At least I know mine's missing. You've been carrying a fake around thinking
> it's the real one. That's worse.

> **NOCTRA:** *(dangerous)* Careful.

> **LUMI:** *(not careful at all)* She's making tea for a dead boy and you've
> been having the same argument with a dead woman for four hundred years, and
> one of you is a grieving kid and the other one is *enormous* and should
> probably know better.

*Silence. The kind with weather in it.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(finally, low)* You have a remarkable talent for saying the worst
> true thing available.

> **LUMI:** People keep telling me that.

> **NOCTRA:** *(turning away)* Yes. They did.

---

## MEETING III — CRYSTAL COAST
### *A record is not the truth*

*Tidal flats gone to glass. Lumi has found something: one crystal column, deep
in a sea-cave, that the Redaction missed. A name is carved in it. It is the only
surviving instance of that name anywhere in Veylora.*

*Noctra is standing in front of it and has been for some time.*

> **LUMI:** That's you. Isn't it.

> **NOCTRA:** *(not moving)* Where did you find it?

> **LUMI:** Under the water. There's a whole gallery down there the Light never
> got into. It's the only one left. I checked the archive, I checked the
> city, I checked — this is *it*. This is the only place in the world your name
> still exists.

> **NOCTRA:** *(reading it)* Hm.

> **LUMI:** *(carefully)* It's got the rest, too. Keeper of the Interval. It
> lists your — it says what you *did*. It says you kept the dark galleries and
> you wove for the Starkeepers and it says —
> *(stopping)*
> It says you warned them.

> **NOCTRA:** Yes.

> **LUMI:** Noctra, this proves it. This is *evidence.* We take this to
> Mirrowen, we put it in front of the council, and four hundred years of —

*Noctra breaks it.*

*One motion. Almost gentle. The column comes apart into gravel and the only
surviving record of his existence goes into the shallows and the tide takes
most of it inside a minute.*

*Lumi does not speak for eleven seconds.*

> **LUMI:** *(very quiet)* Why.

> **NOCTRA:** Because it's not what I want.

> **LUMI:** It's your NAME —

> **NOCTRA:** It's my name in a *rock.*
> *(and now, finally, he raises his voice, and it is terrible)*
> You'd have taken that to Mirrowen and they would have put it behind glass and
> written a card for it. *Noctra. Keeper of the Interval. Circa the Long
> Bright. Donated by the Starkeeper.* And schoolchildren would file past it
> twice a year and read the card and go and have their lunch.

> **LUMI:** That's better than nothing!

> **NOCTRA:** It is EXACTLY nothing.
> *(the violet cracks are flaring; the smoke is coming off him in sheets)*
> I don't want to be **documented**, Keeper.
> I want to be **known**.
> There is a difference and it is the entire difference. A record is a shape
> the truth left behind after it walked off. It is the footprint. It is not the
> foot.
> Four thousand people could read that stone and not one of them would know
> what it was like in there.

*Beat. The tide works at the gravel.*

> **LUMI:** ...What was it like in there?

*And that stops him completely. Because nobody has asked. Not once. In four
hundred years, every single creature has asked what he* wants *or what he* did
*or what he* is going to do *, and this is the first time anyone has asked what
it was* like.

> **NOCTRA:** *(after a very long time, and not loudly)*
> Dark.
> *(beat)*
> I counted for the first eighty years. Then I stopped. Then I started again,
> and I couldn't remember what number I'd been on, so I started from one, and
> that was the worst day.

> **LUMI:** ...I'm sorry.

> **NOCTRA:** Don't.

> **LUMI:** I'm not apologising for it. I didn't do it.
> I'm just sorry.

*Noctra looks at him for a long moment, at a cat who is standing in the wreckage
of the only proof he ever existed and is somehow still the one offering
something.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(quietly, turning to go)* That's the difference. Right there.
> *(beat)*
> Remember it, if you can manage to remember anything.

---

## MEETING IV — EMBER CANYON
### *You are allowed to be angry*

*Black glass and red rock. A year-old fire with no fuel. Noctra is standing too
close to it, which is either significant or a coincidence, and it is not a
coincidence.*

> **LUMI:** They farm it, you know. The people here.

> **NOCTRA:** I know what pyrophytes are.

> **LUMI:** There's a tree up the ridge whose cones are sealed shut with resin
> and the only thing that opens them is a fire. That's it. That's the entire
> plan. It waits its whole life for the worst day and *that's* the day it has
> children.
> Everyone outside the canyon calls this place dead. The people here get really
> angry about that.

> **NOCTRA:** Good.

> **LUMI:** *(sitting)* So Ember asked me a question.

> **NOCTRA:** They do that.

> **LUMI:** It asked what I'm willing to lose. Not sacrifice. *Lose.* Gone,
> nothing back, no lesson.

> **NOCTRA:** *(sharply)* What did you answer?

> **LUMI:** I told it the truth, which is that I don't know what I have.
> *(he holds up the scarf)*
> Bit of an advantage, actually. Turns out you can't be scared of losing things
> when you can't remember owning any.

*Noctra laughs. It is an awful sound — unused, rusted, and genuinely a laugh.
He appears to surprise himself with it.*

> **LUMI:** *(delighted)* Was that a laugh?

> **NOCTRA:** No.

> **LUMI:** That was *absolutely* —

> **NOCTRA:** It was a **respiratory event.**

*Beat. The fire moves. Then Lumi says it, without any particular weight, because
that is the only way it would land:*

> **LUMI:** You're allowed to be angry, you know.

*Noctra goes still.*

> **NOCTRA:** ...What?

> **LUMI:** About whatever it is. The room, the woman, the four hundred years.
> You're allowed to be angry about it.

> **NOCTRA:** *(and he is not being rhetorical; he genuinely does not follow)*
> Why would you say that to me.

> **LUMI:** Because I've been walking behind you for weeks and every single time
> it comes up you go very calm and very quiet and you make a *point*. Like
> you're presenting. Like if you get the argument neat enough somebody's finally
> going to mark it correct.

> **NOCTRA:** ...

> **LUMI:** Nobody's marking it. There's no one left to mark it. You can just be
> furious. That's — that's a whole available option.

*The cracks flare. The gem fracture glints gold. And for a second the thing
standing there is not composed at all.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(and it comes out of him like something tearing)*
> Everyone I have ever spoken to has been AFRAID of my anger or ASHAMED of it
> or used it as the reason I deserved the FLOOR OF A MOUNTAIN FOR FOUR HUNDRED
> YEARS —

> **LUMI:** *(not moving, not flinching)* Yeah.

> **NOCTRA:** — and you sit there and tell me it's *allowed* —

> **LUMI:** It is, though.

*And then the enormous creature made of swallowed light stops, mid-roar, with
nowhere to put any of it, and the fire burns, and nothing happens, and that is
somehow far more devastating than if something had.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(hoarse)* ...You are the single most dangerous thing I have ever
> met.

> **LUMI:** I'm four kilos.

> **NOCTRA:** I am aware of your weight. It has nothing to do with it.

## MEETING V — SKYGARDEN PEAKS
### *Nothing stays where it happened*

*Terraced mountain, hanging gardens, the whole circulation of the world turning
overhead. Ash from Ember is falling here. It has been falling for weeks.*

> **LUMI:** Two towns down there are at war over things they said forty years
> ago. The words only just arrived.

> **NOCTRA:** Sky carries.

> **LUMI:** Sky carries *everything.* That's the whole problem. Nobody here gets
> to have a private moment, it all ends up somewhere else eventually —
> *(catching the ash on a paw)*
> — this is from your canyon. Two hundred miles.

> **NOCTRA:** It isn't my canyon.

> **LUMI:** You spent a week in it.

> **NOCTRA:** *(a fraction too fast)* I was passing through.

> **LUMI:** *(grinning)* Sure.

*Pause. Wind. Then Noctra says the thing he came up here to say, and it is the
cruellest move he makes in the entire story, and he is entirely correct.*

> **NOCTRA:** You've started thinking your kindness is small.

> **LUMI:** What?

> **NOCTRA:** You do it constantly and you apologise for it. You fix a fence.
> You sit with a frightened animal. You tell an enormous monster he's allowed
> to be angry and then you make a joke about your own body weight so that it
> doesn't count.
> You think it's local. You think it stays where you put it.

> **LUMI:** *(uncertain)* ...It's a fence.

> **NOCTRA:** Look up.
> *(the wheel of cloud, turning, the ash crossing two hundred miles)*
> **Nothing stays where it happened.**
> Not one thing. Not ever. That is the verb of this entire region and you are
> standing in the middle of it telling me your kindness is a fence.

> **LUMI:** *(slowly)* ...That's a nice thing to say.

> **NOCTRA:** It isn't, and I'd like you to work out why before I have to say
> it.

*Beat.*

> **LUMI:** ...Because it goes both ways.

> **NOCTRA:** Because it goes both ways.
> Twelve people sat in a warm room and voted, and they went home, and they had
> supper, and they thought it was **small**. They thought it was local. Twelve
> voices and a show of hands and then supper.
> And it is still falling on me. Four hundred years downwind, and it has not
> stopped landing.
> *(beat)*
> Everything arrives, Keeper. That's the good news and it's the entire bill.

---

## MEETING VI — NEON RAINFOREST
### *You have been leaving me messages*

*Saturated bioluminescence. Ten thousand overlapping signals and no dark to read
them against. A researcher's blackout tent glowing at the edge of frame.*

> **LUMI:** They think she's trying to bring back the monster. She's putting out
> lamps in one valley so the fireflies can find each other and they think she's
> — they've got a *word* for it. They call it night-work. Like a crime.

> **NOCTRA:** They would.

> **LUMI:** *(carefully)* You were right. About the lamps. About all of it.
> I read the counts in Mirrowen. The orchards are still down. Nobody knows why.
> It's the moths. It's been the moths for four hundred years and everybody's
> looking at the soil.

*This should be a victory. Noctra takes no pleasure in it whatsoever, and that
is the point.*

> **NOCTRA:** I know.

> **LUMI:** That's it? You've waited four centuries to be told that.

> **NOCTRA:** I have waited four centuries to be **believed**. Being right was
> never in question. I had the counts.
> *(beat)*
> Being right alone in a hole is just arithmetic.

*Then Neon flares, and the whole canopy goes over in one sheet of turquoise, and
in the middle of it Noctra sees something and stops dead.*

> **NOCTRA:** Where did you learn that knot.

> **LUMI:** What knot?

> **NOCTRA:** On the post. At the trailhead. The rope. *Where did you learn it.*

> **LUMI:** *(baffled)* I don't — I do it without thinking, I've been doing it
> since —
> *(looking at his own paws)*
> I do it at every border. Every region. I tie one and I scratch a mark on
> something. I don't know why. I thought it was a trail thing.

> **NOCTRA:** *(very, very quietly)* It isn't a trail thing.

> **LUMI:** Then what is it?

> **NOCTRA:** It's a Nightkeeper's mark. It means *I came through here and I am
> still alive.*
> You leave one at every border. You have left one at **every border**, in a
> language that has been dead for four hundred years, for someone who has been
> walking two days behind you the entire time.

*Long silence. Neon hammering the canopy.*

> **LUMI:** ...Who taught me that?

> **NOCTRA:** *(and he cannot say it, and the not-saying is audible)*
> Someone who is very bad at his job.

> **LUMI:** That's not an answer either.

> **NOCTRA:** No.
> *(turning into the dark, and this is the closest he comes to breaking before
> the Ruins)*
> But you've been signalling to me for two months, Keeper, and you didn't know
> I was there.
> I've read every one of them.

## MEETING VII — FROSTFANG VALLEY
### *You froze one night, four hundred times*

*Aurora. A valley where nothing has moved since the night of the Vault. Lumi has
just come from the vigil house, where a family have been sitting with someone
for four months who can neither recover nor die.*

*He is not okay. This is the first time in the story he is visibly not okay.*

> **NOCTRA:** You told them to let Frost go.

> **LUMI:** I told them what it'd mean.

> **NOCTRA:** You told a family to let their child die.

> **LUMI:** *(flat)* Yes.

> **NOCTRA:** *(not unkind)* And?

> **LUMI:** And they said thank you.
> *(his voice goes)*
> They said *thank you*, Noctra. Four months and I'm the first person who'd
> say the actual sentence out loud and they were **grateful**, and I — I don't
> know how to carry that. I don't have anywhere to put it. I don't have
> anywhere to put anything, that's the whole —

*He stops. Breathes. Gets hold of it.*

> **LUMI:** Frost asked me a question.

> **NOCTRA:** I imagine it did.

> **LUMI:** It asked what I'm keeping that should've been allowed to rot.

> **NOCTRA:** And what did you say?

> **LUMI:** *(and he turns and looks straight at him)*
> I said I'd tell it after I'd told you.

*Beat.*

> **NOCTRA:** Don't.

> **LUMI:** You didn't survive four hundred years.

> **NOCTRA:** **Don't.**

> **LUMI:** You froze **one night**, and then you had it four hundred times.
> *(and he is crying, and he does not stop talking)*
> That's not surviving. That's this valley. That's what's *wrong* with this
> place — nothing here is safe, it's just not finished, everything's held
> exactly where it was on the worst day and it can't heal because healing means
> *changing* and Frost won't allow it —
> and you've been doing it to yourself on purpose since before Mirrowen had
> *streets.*

*Noctra says nothing. The aurora moves. It is very, very quiet.*

> **LUMI:** *(smaller)* I'm not saying let it go. I'm not — I wouldn't. I don't
> think you should let it go. What they did to you was monstrous and it should
> never stop being monstrous.
> I'm saying you're allowed to **grieve** it instead of preserving it.
> Those are different. Grief moves. It's supposed to move.

> **NOCTRA:** *(after a very long time)* And if it moves, what's left?

> **LUMI:** You.

> **NOCTRA:** *(barely audible)* I don't know what that is any more.
> I have been the shape of that night for longer than your entire civilisation
> has had a name for the colour of the sky. Take it out and I am not a person
> with a wound. I am a wound with nothing in it.

*Lumi crosses the snow and sits down next to him. Does not touch him. Just sits,
at a fraction of his size, in the cold.*

> **LUMI:** Then we find out.

> **NOCTRA:** "We."

> **LUMI:** I've got nothing and you've got one thing. Between us that's
> basically a full set.

*And Noctra makes the respiratory event again.*

*The golden fracture on the gem has grown. Neither of them mentions it. Both of
them have noticed.*

## MEETING VIII — CELESTIAL RUINS
### *The truth, and the ninth memory*

*Blank gold-white architecture. Four thousand years of history bleached off
every surface. The Light Star, lucid and refusing, begging not to be taken.*

*And because this is the Star of revelation, and because both of them are
standing in the same room, it does the only thing it knows how to do.*

**It shows them.**

Not a vision. Not a flashback. **Address restoration** — the real mechanism,
finally working at scale, in a building designed for it.

*Lumi gets the ninth quadrant of his life back.*

*And what comes back is not the Vault. Not the office. Not his name.*

*It is a very small cat, sitting in the dark at the edge of Eclipse Sanctuary,
every evening for years, talking to a voice underground.*

---

*When it ends, Lumi is on the floor. Noctra has not moved at all.*

> **LUMI:** *(not looking up)* It was you.

> **NOCTRA:** ...

> **LUMI:** Every night. For *years.* I sat on that rock and you talked to me
> through — how? How were you even —

> **NOCTRA:** Sound travels through stone. It was all I had.

> **LUMI:** You taught me the Refusals.

> **NOCTRA:** Yes.

> **LUMI:** You taught me **four**. Everyone else learns three. Every book says
> three. I've been walking round Veylora quoting a fourth Refusal at people and
> they look at me like I'm —
> *(stopping)*
> *You may not call them back.*

> **NOCTRA:** Yes.

> **LUMI:** You taught me the one rule that makes what you're doing *wrong.*

> **NOCTRA:** **Yes.**

> **LUMI:** Why?!

> **NOCTRA:** *(and it comes out of him flat and immediate, like he has had the
> answer ready for a decade and nobody to give it to)*
> Because somebody had to still know it.
> I was going to come out of that mountain angry. I have always known what I
> was going to be when I came out of that mountain. I have had four hundred
> years to watch myself become it and I could not stop.
> So I built the thing that could.

*Silence.*

> **LUMI:** You made the scarf.

> **NOCTRA:** I made every scarf.

> **LUMI:** You made *this* scarf. You made it in there. In the dark. For me.
> And then you pushed it up through four hundred feet of rock.

> **NOCTRA:** It took eleven years.

> **LUMI:** *(breaking)* You sang to me.

> **NOCTRA:** *(and finally, finally, the composure goes)*
> You were **four weeks old** and you had been left at the edge of the only
> place in the world nothing goes, and there was nobody, and I could not reach
> you, and I could not see you, and the only thing I could do through four
> hundred feet of basalt was **make a noise that wasn't frightening.**
> So I made a noise that wasn't frightening.
> *(beat)*
> For eleven years.

*Lumi is holding the scarf in both paws.*

> **LUMI:** In the Vault. You said find them, and then you said —

> **NOCTRA:** I know what I said.

> **LUMI:** You said *find me.*
> Everyone thinks that was a threat. There's a **song** about it in Mirrowen.
> There's a *children's song* about how the Shadow Cat told the Starkeeper to
> come and find him and now he's waiting to —

> **NOCTRA:** I know what the song says.

> **LUMI:** It wasn't a threat.

> **NOCTRA:** *(and this is the quietest line in the entire story)*
> No.
> I had just watched the only person who has ever known me forget me in front
> of me. In about nine seconds.
> *(beat)*
> Veylora took four hundred years to do that. You managed it before you hit the
> floor.
> *(beat)*
> It wasn't a threat. It was the only thing I could think of that might make
> you come back.

*And the Light Star, which has not spoken to anyone in four centuries, and which
has spent that entire time refusing to ever reveal anything again, chooses this
exact moment to say its one line:*

> **LIGHT:** *...oh.*
> *(beat)*
> *I had forgotten this is also what I am for.*

---

## MEETING IX — ASTRAL WILDS
### *Then it was worth it*

*Not a place. Nine regions visible at once and visibly connected — ash feeding
snow feeding water feeding forest, the whole machine turning.*

*They are not arguing any more. That is what makes this the hardest one.*

> **LUMI:** They're one thing.

> **NOCTRA:** They were always one thing.

> **LUMI:** So when they cut you out — they didn't remove a tenth of it.

> **NOCTRA:** No. You can't remove a tenth of a sentence and keep nine-tenths of
> the meaning. You get a different sentence.
> They've been living in the different sentence for four hundred years and they
> think it's the original.

> **LUMI:** *(quiet)* And you want to run the Convocation and fix it.

> **NOCTRA:** I want to *un-erase* it.

> **LUMI:** By erasing what everyone currently remembers.

> **NOCTRA:** By **correcting** —

> **LUMI:** Noctra.
> Say the actual sentence. You're better than this. Say what it actually does.

*Long pause. And to his enormous credit, he says it.*

> **NOCTRA:** It reaches into every mind in Veylora and changes what is in it,
> without asking, and nobody will ever know it happened.

> **LUMI:** Yeah.

> **NOCTRA:** *(and now the terrible part)*
> And I worked that out two hundred years ago, Keeper. I am not confused. I
> have not made an error you can talk me out of. I sat in the dark and I did
> the arithmetic and I decided it was worth it.
> *(beat)*
> And do you know what the arithmetic was?
> **It was theirs.** Word for word. *This is terrible, and it is the least
> terrible thing available, and we are going to do it to him without asking.*
> That is the exact sentence twelve people used in a warm room.
> I have been rehearsing their argument for four centuries and I never once
> noticed I'd learned it off them.

> **LUMI:** *(very gently)* Then don't say it.

> **NOCTRA:** I have nothing else. It's the only road out of the mountain.

> **LUMI:** It's not a road out. It's the mountain, going in the other
> direction.

*Pause.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(and this is his last real defence)*
> And if I stop? What then. What is the alternative you are offering me.
> Four hundred years, a sealed star, a dead partner, a name that exists nowhere
> and a world that sings a song about how frightening I am to get its children
> indoors.
> If I don't rewrite it, then it stands. Then that's just **what happened**, and
> it stays happened, forever, and I did it all for **nothing.**

> **LUMI:** I know.

> **NOCTRA:** That's not an answer.

> **LUMI:** *(and he doesn't flinch)* No. It's the truth. There isn't a version
> of this where you get it back. It happened. It stands. It was for nothing and
> it was monstrous and nobody is going to give you those four hundred years.

> **NOCTRA:** ...

> **LUMI:** But I remember you.

> **NOCTRA:** One person.

> **LUMI:** One person is how it works. That's *always* how it works. You didn't
> want to be documented, you said it yourself — you wanted to be **known.**
> A Convocation can put your name in every book in Veylora and not one of those
> people will know what it was like in there.
> I do. I asked.
> *(beat)*
> You can have a world that's been made to say your name, or you can have
> somebody who came and found you. You can't have both, and you've known which
> one you actually wanted since Crystal Coast, because you smashed the other one
> with your own paw.

*Long, long silence. The whole machine turning above them.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(finally)* ...That's a very small thing to have waited four
> hundred years for.

> **LUMI:** Yeah.
> *(beat)*
> Was it worth it?

*And Noctra looks at him — a small white cat in a scarf he wove by hand in the
dark, humming, without noticing, a lullaby written by a prisoner for an
orphan — and he does not answer, because the answer is embarrassing, and
because they both already know it.*

## MEETING X — ECLIPSE SANCTUARY
### *The last argument, which is not a fight*

*Black basalt. Four hundred years of no light. The looms still standing. One of
them still has work on it.*

*And the tally marks. A very great many, then a long stretch of none, then they
start again.*

*Lumi has nine Stars. Nine fastenings lit. The tenth is empty and always has
been.*

> **NOCTRA:** You know what's in me.

> **LUMI:** Yeah.

> **NOCTRA:** Then you know how this ends. There are two ways to get a sealed
> Star out of a living thing and one of them is very quick.

> **LUMI:** I'm not going to do that.

> **NOCTRA:** You *should.* Nine Stars and the Shadow makes ten. You could hold
> the Convocation yourself, tonight. Put everything back. Fix ten regions. Give
> a valley its winter and a canyon its harvest and a family their funeral.
> One motion, Keeper. Everything you have wanted since you woke up in a field.
> *(beat)*
> And you'd get your memory back. All of it. Every name. That's not a
> side-effect, that's the *whole* —

> **LUMI:** I know what it is.

> **NOCTRA:** Then why are you standing there.

*And Lumi does the thing nobody in four hundred years has done.*

*He takes off the scarf.*

*Nine lit Stars. Woven night. He holds it out, at the full stretch of both
forelegs, to the creature who made it.*

> **LUMI:** Because it's yours.

> **NOCTRA:** ...What.

> **LUMI:** You wove it. You put every fastening on it. You gave it to a stray
> because there wasn't anyone else and you couldn't even see him.
> It's not mine. I never owned it. That's the *first Refusal*, and you're the
> one who taught it to me, and I have been carrying your work around Veylora for
> four years calling it mine.
> *(beat)*
> Take it.

> **NOCTRA:** *(not moving)* If I take that, I win.

> **LUMI:** Yes.

> **NOCTRA:** Nine Stars. And the tenth is already in my chest.

> **LUMI:** Yes.

> **NOCTRA:** *Why are you doing this.*

> **LUMI:** Because you've spent four hundred years being something somebody
> *did to you*, and I'm not going to be another one. I'm not taking it off you.
> I'm not making the choice for you. Nobody's ever *asked* you, Noctra. They
> voted, and she read from a page, and the council decided, and Veylora decided,
> and you decided in the dark on your own with nobody to check it against.
> *(and he pushes the scarf further out)*
> I'm asking.
> You can have the whole thing. Right now. Nobody's stopping you.

*And the Keeper of the Interval stands in the dark he was buried in, with
everything he has wanted for four centuries held out at arm's length by
somebody who is not even slightly protecting himself.*

*And the golden fracture across the gem goes all the way through.*

> **NOCTRA:** *(and it is barely a voice at all)*
> ...That's not fair.

> **LUMI:** No.

> **NOCTRA:** You learned that from me as well.

> **LUMI:** *(and he is smiling, and crying)* Yeah. You were really good at your
> job.

> **SHADOW:** *Do you want me back — or do you want him back?*

> **LUMI:** I want you to stop being the same question.
