# FurEver — *The Lost Starkeeper*

Story bible, dialogue scripts, and an anime teaser-trailer prompt pack for the
world of **Veylora**.

## The pitch

A small white cat who has forgotten everything walks across a broken world to
collect ten stars that left him on purpose — pursued by the only creature alive
who remembers who he is, and who cannot tell him, because being told is not the
same as remembering.

## The spine

> **The cure for being erased cannot be erasing someone else.**
>
> **You are not your memories. You are what your memories made you — and that is
> the part that survives them.**
>
> **Being carried is not the same as being chosen.**
